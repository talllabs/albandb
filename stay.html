Check-in & House Guide

Address

4314 Marina City Drive, PH18
Marina del Rey, California 90292
United States

Building Management and Security (at the gate) are onsite and available should there be issues/concerns with building/grounds (e.g., elevators).

Arrival & Parking

1. Security Gate

  • Check-in at the Security Gate.
  • Give the guard your name and state you are going to PH18.
  • Proceed straight ahead towards the Center Tower for parking.

2. Parking

  • Enter the Tower and Turn Left at the Stop Sign.
  • Look for your spot on your IMMEDIATE right.
  • Space #258 (You can move the “No Parking” sign).
  • Alternative: Just a couple of spots down is Space #264.

3. Elevator Access

Directly across from the parking is the elevator to the Penthouse (PH). When you arrive at the Penthouse level, take a left from the elevator lobby and head to PH18.

Access Code

Touch keypad and hold until the numbers illuminate. Enter the code below and press the circular button at the end.

98765432

Door Tips

If you have difficulty opening the door, make sure it is closed tight by pulling the door towards you to engage the latch, then try inputting the code again.

Important: Locking Logic

  • Automatic Lock: The LOCKLY keypad automatically locks itself. Do not manually turn the lock from the outside.
  • Exiting: Manually unlock by turning the top LOCKLY knob to the unlocked position. It will re-lock automatically a few seconds after you exit. Ensure the door is securely shut.

House Details

Shades

***Please DO NOT open/close the shades. Leave them in the open position.***

Patio

Remember to close the patio door when you leave.

Air Conditioning / Heat

There are thermostats on both levels. For downstairs, the thermostat is on the exterior lower bathroom wall under the stairs. Do not use the thermostat near the windows on the downstairs level.

Cleaning

The unit has been professionally cleaned for your arrival. Additional cleaning services are available for a flat rate of $250. Please let us know if you need this.

Amenities

Coffee / Cafe

  • There is no coffee maker in the unit, but there is an electric kettle in the cupboard left of the refrigerator.
  • On-site Cafe: Offers coffee, snacks, sandwiches, etc.
  • Directions to Cafe: After exiting the elevator into the parking garage, take a right from the elevator lobby, a second right to exit the parking garage, and a third right at the stop sign. Follow the sidewalk.

Pool

Just past the cafe on the left (catty-corner) you will see stairs leading up to the pool. No key is needed for access.

Laundry & Towels

In-unit laundry is located at the top of the stairs in the closet. Fresh towels are in the drawers. You may use the laundry as needed.

Linens

Can be left on the bed after your departure. House cleaning will strip and wash after your stay.

Trash & Recycling

  • In-Unit trash is below the sink area.
  • Please empty trash and recycling at the end of your stay.
  • Trash Room: Located off of the elevator lobby behind the “Electrical Room” door.

Shopping

Marina Waterside Shopping Center: (4700 Admiralty Way). Features Ralph’s Grocery and Mendocino Farms. Complimentary parking available via Fiji Way, Admiralty Way, or Mindanao Way.
