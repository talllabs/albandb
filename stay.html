Arrival & Parking

Gate Check-In

  • Give security your name and say you are going to PH18.
  • Proceed straight ahead toward Center Tower.

Parking

  • Enter the tower area and turn LEFT at the stop sign.
  • Primary spaces: #258 or #264.
  • You may move the temporary “No Parking” sign if placed there.

Building Access

  • Elevator is directly across from parking.
  • Select Penthouse / PH level.
  • Exit elevator, turn LEFT, proceed to PH18.

Door Entry

Touch keypad and hold until numbers illuminate.
